# Pushed Rolls

## Rule Summary

After a failed skill roll I can push it once, if I can say what my investigator does differently. Fail the pushed roll and the Keeper picks something bad.

## When This Comes Up

Searching, persuading, climbing. Not combat or Sanity rolls.

## Procedure

1. Fail the roll.
2. Describe the extra effort and agree it with the Keeper.
3. Roll again. On a failure, the Keeper chooses the consequence.

## Important Modifiers or Exceptions

- Can't push a pushed roll.
- Can't spend Luck on a pushed roll.
