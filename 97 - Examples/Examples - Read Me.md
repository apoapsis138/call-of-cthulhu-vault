# Examples - Read Me

One filled-in note of every type, from a made-up 1923 scenario: Margaret Ashby, a Boston reporter, looks for the missing antiquarian Silas Crane in the fictional fishing town of Greyhaven, Maine, where a bell rings out on the flats at low tide.

They show what a finished note looks like, how the properties link up, and what the Connections panels, dashboards, timeline and Vault Maintenance do once there are notes to work with.

Because they sit here and not in their type folders, Metadata Menu's dropdowns don't apply to them; in a real campaign each note lives in its own folder.

All of them have `dg-publish: false` and are excused from the publishing check, so they never reach the site.

**Delete this whole folder when the real campaign starts.** A few names (Boston, United States) are linked but have no note, on purpose: they show up under Notes to create in Vault Maintenance.
